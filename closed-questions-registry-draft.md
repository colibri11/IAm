# Draft Registry of Philosophical Blockers to Be Closed

This document is a draft union of three research corpora:
- English + Spanish
- German + French
- Russian + Chinese

At this stage, duplicates are intentionally preserved. The purpose of this file is not yet to provide a final taxonomy, but to keep the full raw registry in one place before deduplication and regrouping.
